How to Get Access to Gemini 4 Argon

Last checked: October 5, 2026 · Independent tracker, not affiliated with Google · Status is re-checked daily

Short answer

You can't buy or sign up for Gemini 4 Argon yet. Since its announcement on September 30, 2026, Google has limited access to vetted organizations in its Fairwind Program, a cyber-defense partnership.

Google says the next groups will be paid Gemini API customers and Google AI Ultra subscribers. It has not given a date. Subscribing to Ultra today does not unlock Argon.

Right now the only form you can fill in is the Fairwind interest form, and it is meant for organizations, not individuals.

Applying to the Fairwind Program

Fairwind is Google DeepMind's early-access program for cyber defenders. It has over 650 partners. A subset of them get Gemini 4 Argon, which they can use on its own or inside CodeMender, Google's code-security agent. Google is giving these partners Argon without cyber guardrails, so admission is strict.

Who Google prioritizes

  • Governments and national cyber authorities
  • Critical infrastructure operators: healthcare, telecommunications, energy and financial networks
  • Core technology platforms whose software is used by millions of downstream users
  • Academic labs doing defensive benchmarking (students are pointed to CodeMender on Google Cloud instead)

What you agree to if accepted

  • Background checks on your organization's security history and ethical record
  • User-level authentication, phishing-resistant MFA and access controls
  • Argon access only for internal security, incident response or penetration testing teams, with per-employee tracking
  • Only permitted dual-use work: authorized threat simulation, reverse engineering, malware analysis for defense or research
  • No sharing, redistributing or reselling access

How long it takes

Google gives no timeline. Its FAQ says it will "review and respond to eligible partners who meet our criteria as soon as we can." If you are an individual developer or a company without a security mission, this path is very unlikely to work.

How to tell the rollout has started

  • API: an Argon model ID appears in the Gemini API model list and in Google AI Studio's model picker. As of Oct 5 the newest listed IDs are still in the 3.8 family.
  • Ultra: Argon shows up in the Gemini app's model menu for Ultra accounts.
  • Google blog: a follow-up post on blog.google announcing availability.

This page is re-checked daily and the table above changes when any of these happen.

Be careful with "Argon access" offers

Fairwind partners are not allowed to share, redistribute or sell access. Until Google opens the API, a third-party site or reseller selling Gemini 4 Argon access is either breaking those terms or not offering the real model.

What to use in the meantime

  • Security work: Google says organizations that don't qualify for Fairwind can still use CodeMender with publicly available models.
  • Gemini API: the current models in the model list. Code written against the Gemini API should only need a model ID change once Argon ships there.
  • Other frontier models you can use today: OpenAI's GPT-6 Astra on paid ChatGPT plans and the OpenAI API, and Anthropic's Claude Fable 5.

Announced pricing

Introductory API price: $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens. Cached input tokens are 95% off. Output is capped at 1M tokens per response, up from 64K. Google hasn't said how Argon fits into consumer plans beyond Ultra going first. Google AI Ultra pricing varies by country; see Google's subscriptions page for yours (in Singapore it starts at SGD 139.99 per month).

FAQ

Can I use Gemini 4 Argon in the Gemini app?

Not yet. Ultra subscribers are first in line for the consumer rollout, but no date has been given.

Is there a waitlist?

No consumer or developer waitlist has been announced. The only form linked from Google's Argon pages is the Fairwind interest form, which is for organizations.

Will upgrading to Google AI Ultra now get me early access?

It puts you in the first consumer group once the rollout starts. It does not unlock Argon today.

Can individuals or students apply to Fairwind?

Fairwind is for organizations. Academic labs doing defensive benchmarking can apply. Google points students to CodeMender on Google Cloud.

Is Gemini 4 Argon on Vertex AI or Gemini Enterprise?

Google says Fairwind partners can access it as a managed model on Gemini Enterprise with zero data retention. General enterprise availability has not been announced.
